# Week 10 Result Summary - PASCAL VOC2007 Benchmark

## Scope

Week 10 uses the PASCAL VOC2007 trainval/test benchmark split.

## Compared groups

1. Baseline YOLOv9-S + NMS
2. Proposed V10DualDDetect + NMS
3. Proposed V10DualDDetect + no-NMS

## Dataset

- Source: PASCAL VOC2007
- Train: VOC2007 trainval
- Val/Test: VOC2007 test
- Classes: 20
- Format: VOC XML converted to YOLO txt
- Difficult objects: excluded by default

## Interpretation

This is appropriate as an undergraduate thesis-level benchmark experiment. It should be reported as VOC2007 benchmark-scale evidence, not COCO evidence.
